KANO DIRECT ENTRY

SCREENING (BUKDES)

ONLINE REGISTRATION

Eligible candidates need to register online for the Bayero University Kano Direct Entry Screening (BUKDES) exercise.

Step 1: Login

Login using your DE Number, State of Origin and Gender.

Step 2: Profile Update

Applicants are to provide their Date of Birth, LGA, Phone Number used when registering with JAMB, Email address used when registering with JAMB, alternate Phone Number (if any), alternate Email address (if any), Next of Kin, Phone Number of next of kin and Contact Address.

Applicants cannot change their full name and state of origin.

Step 3: O' Level Result

Candidates are required to provide their O' Level examination details and results.

Step 4: Payment

Candidates should complete the required screening payment before proceeding to the next stage.

Step 5: Acknowledgement Slip

After successful completion of the required stages, candidates can generate and print their acknowledgement slip.
