Welcome to Undergraduate Screening Exercise Engine

Candidates are advised to carefully read the instructions below before proceeding.

Instructions

Students are expected to complete the required screening procedures through the portal.

1

JAMB Admission

Candidates should clear with JAMB by accepting and printing their JAMB Admission Letter where applicable.

2

Login to the Portal

Login using your JAMB Number, State of Origin and Gender to access your screening account.

3

Admission Information

Eligible candidates can access the relevant admission information and required documents through the portal.

4

Payment

Where applicable, candidates can generate the personalised payment information and proceed through the approved payment channel.

5

Document Upload

Candidates may be required to upload relevant documents in the specified format and size.

  • JAMB Slip
  • JAMB Admission Letter
  • O' Level Result
  • A' Level Result where applicable
  • Other relevant documents
6

Acknowledgement Slip

After completion and verification of the required stages, candidates can print their acknowledgement slip.

Important Notice

Candidates are advised to carefully enter their information and ensure that all information supplied is correct before submission.

For enquiries or complaints, use the designated support channel provided by the University.
